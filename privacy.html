Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Talqo iOS app (com.voiceqr.app) and this support website.

Your voice stays on your device

Recording, audio compression, QR generation and QR decoding happen on your device. Talqo does not upload your recordings or QR audio payloads to a developer-operated server. You do not need a Talqo account. The app does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting services.

Permissions and files

  • Microphone access is used when you record a message.
  • Camera access is used when you scan a QR code.
  • You can choose an audio file or QR image to import. Talqo processes the selected file and may create a local working copy.
  • When you choose to save a QR image, the app requests permission to add it to Photos. Audio and document exports are saved or shared only through actions you initiate.

You can change camera, microphone and Photos permissions in iOS Settings.

Sharing a voice QR

The audio is contained in the QR itself. Anyone who obtains the QR and uses Talqo may listen to it. A name you add to a card is visible on the exported image. Treat a voice QR like a recording you are sharing: send it only to intended recipients.

Talqo cannot revoke an exported QR or erase copies held by another person. Apps or services you choose in the share sheet handle the shared file under their own privacy policies.

Purchases and local balances

Apple processes purchases and verifies subscription transactions. Talqo does not receive your payment-card details. The app stores free-use counts, purchased generation credits and processed transaction identifiers in this device’s secure Keychain storage to maintain balances and prevent duplicate grants.

Credits are not synchronized between devices. Active subscriptions can be restored through Apple. Keychain data may remain after uninstalling the app; device replacement, erasure or loss of that data can affect a local credit balance.

Retention and deletion

Recordings and working files are processed locally. Exported files remain wherever you save or share them until you delete them there. Removing local app data does not remove exported files, recipients’ copies, or Apple’s purchase records. We do not hold a server-side copy of your recordings to delete.

This website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and request information to provide and secure its hosting service. We do not add tracking scripts, advertising cookies or analytics to these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you contact us using GitHub Issues, your issue and attachments are public. GitHub processes your account information under its own policies. Do not include private recordings, sensitive QR codes, passwords, transaction identifiers or payment details. We use the information you choose to provide to investigate and answer your request.

Changes and privacy questions

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date below. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you posted in support, contact the Talqo developer. Do not post sensitive personal information.
