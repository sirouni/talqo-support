THE SOUND IS IN THE CODE

No audio-hosting subscription to keep it playing.

Your recording is processed on your device and stored in the QR itself. Exported codes remain playable with Talqo even after your generation subscription ends. No Talqo account is required.

Recipients need the Talqo app. Audio is compressed, so preview it before sharing. Anyone with the QR and Talqo may listen; share personal messages thoughtfully.
